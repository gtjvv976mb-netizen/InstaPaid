Questions in the comments

?Where do the name and picture come from?

From the post you comment under. We look at its photo and caption and name the coin after them, with a ticker of 2 to 10 letters. The coin's picture is the post's photo (for a video or Reel, its cover), and its link on pump.fun opens that post.

If we can't read the post, the name comes from the caption's first words or the creator's username. A post that shares no picture with us gets the InstaPaid coin.

?Can I add lore?

Yes. Write it after the command: @instapaid.official make a token for this creator: the king of sunsets. It becomes the coin's description on pump.fun.

Links are taken out and it's kept under 400 characters. We leave it out if it's hateful, sexual, harassing or defamatory, pretends to be someone, or promises price, profit or returns, and if we can't check it. Without lore the description stays empty: we never write lore for you.

?Where do you post new coins?

On our own Instagram feed, @instapaid.official. We post new coins there and mention the creator, so they know a coin exists and can claim its fees. We post a limited number a day and at most one post per creator a month, and a coin launched on the website only once its name and picture pass our check, so not every coin gets a post.

For a comment launch we also reply under the comment that asked, with the coin's link and the creator's claim page.

?Who gets the fees?

The creator the coin was made for. pump.fun's creator fees from every coin made for a handle build up in one vault for that handle. The fan who launched it never gets them.

Until the creator claims, that vault is kept by InstaPaid. If InstaPaid ever takes a share, the claim page shows it before anything is sent.

?How does a creator claim?

Open instapaid.fun/claim and get a one-time code (it lasts 15 minutes). DM it to @instapaid.official from your account, then enter any Solana wallet and the fees are sent.

The first claim ties the vault to your account for good, even if you change your username later. We never ask for your password, seed phrase or private key, and never ask you to pay to claim.

?What does it cost?

Commenting is free: we pay the launch. On the website you pay pump.fun's launch cost and network fees, plus any first buy you choose, from your own wallet. We pay the network fee when a creator claims.

?Does it work on any post?

Public posts only. The comment has to @mention @instapaid.official; the word alone sends us nothing. Private accounts and Stories don't reach us.

A creator who already has a coin doesn't get another by comment; asking again gets a reply pointing to it. If launches are paused for the day, the reply says so.

?Is this official?

No. InstaPaid is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. Coins are made by fans, and a creator hasn't endorsed one unless they say so themselves.

?I'm a creator and don't want coins

DM @instapaid.official from your account and tell us. We stop new coins in your name, by comment and on the website, and stop posting about you; anyone who asks after that gets a reply saying you'd rather not.

A coin already on pump.fun can't be deleted by anyone, but its fees stay yours: you can claim them or leave them.

?What if a username changes hands?

Before the first claim, whoever holds the handle when they verify can claim it. After that the vault stays with the account that claimed, through renames and recycled handles. Each creator's page shows whether their vault has been claimed.

Very small balances (below about 0.001 SOL) can't be sent yet. If that happens, come back when more has built up.

?Is it financial advice?

No. Meme coins are speculative and can lose all their value. Nothing here is financial advice, and nothing here says any coin will go up. Only use money you can afford to lose.
